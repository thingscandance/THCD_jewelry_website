Sizing

How to Find
Your Ring Size

01

Option 1 — Paper Strip

  1. Cut a strip of paper about 10cm long and 1cm wide.
  2. Wrap it around the finger you want to size, where the ring will sit.
  3. Mark where the ends meet, snug but not too tight.
  4. Lay it flat and measure the length in mm. This is your finger circumference.
  5. Match it to the size chart below.
02

Option 2 — Use a Ring You Own

  1. Grab a ring that fits the finger you're sizing.
  2. Place it flat on a ruler.
  3. Take a clear photo from directly above — not at an angle.
  4. Measure the inner diameter in mm.
  5. Match it to the chart below.
