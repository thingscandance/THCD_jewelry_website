strobe/media?Drop photos and videos here, or press choose files. Drag a frame onto another to change the order; × removes it. Click a frame to put it into the cut; frames go in the order you click. Tags on each frame: sheet puts the frame on the contact sheet (with none tagged, the sheet takes a random handful). logo on product means this frame already shows your logo, so the overlay hides and a deep hit sounds; end makes it the final packshot; hiss adds a hiss on that cut. strobe/search?Find frames by describing them in English, for example a man jumping at night or silver ring on black. Press find: every frame gets a rank number, and the four best glow pink. add top 4 to cut puts those four into the cut. strobe/script?Write the reel as a list, one beat per line, in English: night facade, man jumping, silver ring, end: ring on black. build the cut from the script picks the closest frame for every line, in that order, without repeating a frame. A line that starts with end: becomes the packshot. The grey lines in the box are only an example; type your own.one beat per line
each line picks the closest frame; start a line with "end:" for the packshot
strobe/cut?The order of your reel. Drag frames to reorder, double-click to take one out. If nothing is picked, all frames are used in upload order. another edit keeps the frames and reshuffles rhythm, splits, punch-ins and glitches.—

strobe/settings?Your logo (typed or a file) and its font, the small captions in the corners, how many frames each still holds, how much glitch and of which kind, the look of the whole reel, and which devices are used.
