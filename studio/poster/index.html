POSTER

Club-poster type that always fits its frame, people cut out of your photos, printed in riso, xerox or halftone, and a collage that moves in a seamless loop.

poster/photosno photos
?Full frame uses each photo whole. Cut out the person finds the person in every photo and lifts them off the background, so type can go behind them, contour lines can follow the body and pieces can be collaged. The first cut-out downloads a model once (about 45 MB); after that it is quick. A PNG that already has a transparent background is used as it is.
drop photos here, or

In the preview: drag a photo to move it, scroll to zoom it, double-click to reset. The order here is the order on the poster. If a cut-out caught a piece of wall or floor, press erase on its tile and paint over that piece in the preview; undo brings the original back.

poster/text— ?Every piece of text has its own frame on the poster and is sized to fill it, so nothing runs off the edge. Big lines grow to the full width. fits: all good. at minimum: the frame made it as small as it may go, a shorter text will look better. cut: it did not fit even condensed, the end is hidden with "…". Switch on "show text frames" under the preview to see the frames.
poster/layout
?When a big word is too short to fill its frame, its letters grow taller (up to about two times), like stretched type on club posters. Off keeps the font's own proportions.
?Small labels put a tiny dim word above each piece of small print, like a form. Scatter words drops a few faint mono words into empty space, the THCD annotation texture; they never cover real text. Text up the sides turns place, date and small print into thin vertical lines along the edges.
poster/photo look

Every print look keeps a clear version of the photo underneath; print strength sets how much texture goes on top. In riso the two inks drift out of register and back over the loop.

poster/motion
?With two or more photos, FIGURE, BEHIND, SPLIT and STACK cut from one photo to the next on the beat while the type stays where it is.

presets?A preset keeps the whole look: layout, format, colours, fonts, photo look, edge, echo, motion. Photos are never saved in it. Tick with text to keep the words too. Saved presets stay in this browser; save file gives you a .json to keep or send, open file loads one.
