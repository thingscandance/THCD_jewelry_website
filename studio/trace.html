studio/notes

Clear glass, a living cap, a pale heroine. The product leaves its shape on her cheek: the trace the brand is named after.

  1. 01Paper world: the carton, the fold, the bottle. The logo locks onto the deboss and the etched glass.
  2. 02A white flash, then the cold world: membrane, skin, the bottle cut in half.
  3. 03The bottle leaves her cheek; its outline stays.
  4. 04Packshot on paper. A glass ping, then black.
