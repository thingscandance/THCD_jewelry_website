about this project

WRP reads as “wrap”. The cover wraps the bar, the city wraps the rider. Everything soft here is made to get through something hard, which is the whole brand in four words: soft shell, hard city.

The film follows that line. A rider comes out of the sun through streets upholstered in quilted nylon, drifts, and throws water into the lens. Then the city drops away and only the object is left in a grey studio, where the zip opens and the HUD names each part.

Detail is the story. Every callout comes from the product itself: the mohair cuff, the snap strap, the zip on the underside. No mood without a part number.
