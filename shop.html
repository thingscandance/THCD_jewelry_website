Everything is measured right here in your browser, on a phone or a laptop. No camera, no app, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to email the result.

Hold a regular bank card upright against the screen next to the rectangle (on a laptop, lay it flat on the screen). Move the slider until the rectangle matches the card exactly. Every bank card is 85.6 × 54 mm.

Saved for this device, so you only do it once per screen.

Put the ring on the screen and move the slider until the pink circle matches the ring's inner edge, not the outer one.

17.0 mm

Measure in the evening if you can: fingers are a little larger by then, so the size fits more reliably.

  1. Cut a paper strip about 10 cm long and 1 cm wide.
  2. Wrap it around the base of the finger you will wear the ring on.
  3. Mark where it overlaps: snug against the skin, not tight.
  4. Lay it flat and measure to the mark in millimetres. That is the circumference.
